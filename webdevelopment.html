Simple, transparent project terms

We agree on deliverables before development begins.

Payment schedule
50% to start, 30% after design/layout approval, and 20% before launch or final handover.
Revisions
Each package includes the stated revision rounds. New features and major scope changes are quoted separately.
Third-party costs
Domain, hosting, paid plugins/themes, stock assets, subscriptions and gateway fees are paid by the client unless listed in the quote.
Maintenance & support
Ongoing maintenance is not included unless specified. New features are separate from routine care.
Handover & ownership
Agreed files and credentials are handed over after full payment. Project timing starts once the deposit and required content are received.
Taxes
Prices are indicative and exclusive of applicable taxes. Confirm tax treatment in the formal quotation.
